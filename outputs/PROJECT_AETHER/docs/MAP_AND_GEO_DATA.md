# Map and geospatial data decision

Reviewed 2026-10-17 (Asia/Seoul). **No external terrain, imagery, airport database, map service, or asset provider is adopted or bundled.** The current test world is synthetic. Geodetic coordinate support alone does not supply real terrain. This is an engineering screening record, not legal certification; the applicable product license and account agreement control any future use.

| Candidate | Potential role | Conditions and implementation consequence | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cesium for Unreal | Unreal geospatial renderer and 3D Tiles client | Plugin is Apache 2.0; this does not license hosted terrain or imagery. See the [official FAQ](https://cesium.com/learn/unreal/unreal-faq/). | Candidate; Unreal unavailable |
| Cesium ion / hosted content | Streaming, terrain, tiling | Account plan, asset terms and attribution apply separately. Current terms distinguish internal use from commercial sublicensing/integration. Do not assume a standard subscription covers a distributed simulator. [ion terms](https://cesium.com/legal/terms-of-service), [content guide](https://cesium.com/learn/ion/content-usage-and-attribution-guide/). | Not enabled |
| Google Map Tiles / Photorealistic 3D Tiles | Visual scenery | API billing and credentials required. Restricted prefetch/storage/cache/offline/extraction; implement permitted HTTP caching and visible Google/provider attribution. Collision meshes, elevation extraction and offline scenery cannot be assumed authorized. Evaluate the actual intended visualization and agreement; this record makes no blanket assertion that all games are prohibited. [Policies](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/tile/policies?hl=en), [billing](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/tile/usage-and-billing). | Not enabled |
| OpenStreetMap data | Roads, building footprints, land-use context | ODbL: attribution and applicable database share-alike obligations; derived databases and produced works require separate analysis. Open data rights do not grant unrestricted use of community tile servers. [Copyright/license](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). Standard raster tile service prohibits bulk/offline prefetch. [Tile policy](https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/tiles/). | Not imported |
| OurAirports | Airport/runway discovery metadata | Provider releases data to the public domain without accuracy/fitness warranty. Credit is requested, not mandatory. Retain provenance and validate records; this is not certified navigation data. [Official downloads/terms](https://ourairports.com/data/). | Not imported |
| USGS 3DEP | Regional elevation, principally US coverage | USGS states 3DEP products are free and without use restrictions. Record each product's metadata, acquisition date, resolution, horizontal/vertical datum and coverage; do not apply this conclusion to every third-party layer displayed by a USGS viewer. [3DEP program](https://www.usgs.gov/3d-elevation-program). | Not imported |
| Copernicus DEM GLO-30-F / GLO-90-F | Global elevation baseline | Product-specific free licenses include required source/modification and liability notices. They are not public-domain dedications. EEA-10 and restricted product variants have different eligibility/terms. [Product page](https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/explore-data/data-collections/copernicus-contributing-missions/collections-description/COP-DEM), [license collection](https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/sites/default/files/media/files/2025-06/copernicus_contributing_mission_data_access_v2_cop_dem_licenses.pdf). | Not imported |

Copernicus DEM represents a **surface model**, including vegetation/buildings, rather than a universally bare-earth terrain model. Its published vertical reference is EGM2008. An importer must convert elevations explicitly before mixing them with ellipsoidal heights; copying height numbers directly is insufficient. [Dataset specifications](https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/explore-data/data-collections/copernicus-contributing-missions/collections-description/COP-DEM).

Before enabling a provider, record the exact product/release, source URL, license version, acquisition date, bounds, resolution, datum, checksum, attribution text, redistribution permissions, retention rules and budget. Keep data provenance separate from renderer/plugin licenses. Use a small authorized region first; no whole-Earth download is scheduled. Recheck eligibility and notices for the selected Copernicus product rather than applying generic Sentinel terms.

The initial integration preference is a small independently downloadable elevation dataset plus explicit airport provenance. That is a future engineering direction, not a provider adoption or a claim that coverage has been tested.
